# FIST OF DOOM: BRUTAL BRAWLER

A first-person 3D retro-modern arena fighting game inspired by *Doom* and *Duke Nukem*, featuring pure visceral melee combat (no guns), smashable supply crates with weapon upgrades, and fully articulated **3D rival human fighters**.

---

## 🥊 3D Human Rival Fighters (Like Other Players!)

The 2D flat cartoon sprites have been completely replaced with **articulated 3D humanoid combatants**:

- **Bruiser Tyson**: Heavyweight boxer with taped red fists, red boxing trunks, high-impact hooks, and tanky vitality.
- **Striker Damian**: Agile street fighter with red headband, combat cargo pants, cyan hand wraps, and rapid flurry jabs.
- **Cyber Rex**: Arena cyborg brawler in dark armor with golden brass knuckle wraps, neon green optic visor, and power crosses.
- **Iron Kai**: Muay Thai contender with purple shorts, white taped fists, and spiky topknot.

### Combat Behaviors & Human Animations:
- **Locomotion Walk Cycle**: Legs swing in tandem with knee bends, arms pump, and torso bobs and sways naturally.
- **Boxer Guard & Bobbing**: Fists raised high protecting their chin when near the player.
- **Dynamic Punch Combos**: Telegraphed left jabs and right crosses where arms extend and torso twists into each strike.
- **Hit Reactions & Concussed Stagger**: Head and torso snap back on hit; at low health (<32% HP), fighters enter a concussed stagger state primed for an instant Glory Knockout execution!
- **Knockdown Physics**: When KO'd, fighters crumple and fall backward onto the arena mat.
- **Overhead 3D Status Bars**: Floating dynamic nameplates and health bars above each fighter.

## 🎮 Controls

- **Move & Strafe**: `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`
- **Look & Aim**: `Mouse` (or Arrow Keys `←` `→` `↑` `↓` in Free Mouse Mode)
- **Primary Attack**: `Left Click` or `F`
- **Mighty Boot Kick**: `Right Click` or `E`
- **Jump**: `Spacebar`
- **Rush / Sprint**: `Shift`
- **💥 METEOR JUMP SLAM**: `Spacebar` (while airborne) + `Attack` (Massive radial ground-pound shockwave)
- **Select Attacks**: `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`
- **Release Mouse / Pause**: `ESC` or `P` (or click top left button)

## 📦 Supply Crate Weapon Upgrades

Smash crates around the arena to unlock:
1. **Bare Knuckles [1]**: Fast standard jabs.
2. **Brass Knuckles [2]**: Heavy bone-crushing thunder hook.
3. **Combat Boots [3]**: Mighty boot kick that sends rivals flying.
4. **Meteor Gauntlet [4]**: Devastating airborne jump slam with radial ground shockwave.
5. **Berserk Stimpacks & Shields**: Vitality recovery and 3x melee damage Blood Rage mode.
